A short, practical book about talking to customers without fooling yourself. The core idea: people will lie to you to be nice, so ask questions that make lying hard.

**Three rules I kept:**

- Talk about their life, not your idea.
- Ask about specifics in the past, not generics or opinions about the future.
- Talk less, listen more.

The line I underlined twice: compliments are the fool's gold of customer learning. A conversation that ends with "sounds great, let me know when it launches" taught you nothing.

What I'm changing: ending every interview by asking what they've already tried, and what it cost them.
