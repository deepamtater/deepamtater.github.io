I've tried every productivity system. The one that stuck is the smallest. Every Sunday, twenty minutes, one page:

1. What did I actually spend the week on?
2. What would I do differently with the same week again?
3. What's the one thing that would make next week a good one?

The trick is question two. It turns the review from a to-do list into a feedback loop. Most weeks the answer is boring — sleep earlier, say no to one meeting — and that's the point.
